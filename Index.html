How SmartCampus AI Works

A complete workflow connecting IoT devices, intelligent decision making, energy automation and predictive maintenance.

Problem Statement

Educational campuses consume significant amounts of electricity through classrooms, laboratories, offices and other facilities. Energy is often wasted when appliances remain active in unoccupied spaces. At the same time, equipment failures are frequently handled through manual complaints, causing delays in identifying, assigning and resolving maintenance issues. The proposed Smart Campus Automation System addresses both problems through real-time IoT monitoring, intelligent energy automation, automatic fault detection, predictive maintenance, and automated employee assignment.

Solution Statement

The system continuously monitors campus environments and devices using IoT sensors and software-based monitoring. When abnormal conditions are detected, it automatically identifies the affected device and location, determines the responsible department, prioritizes the issue and assigns an available employee. For energy management, the system analyzes occupancy, environmental conditions and consumption patterns to automatically control suitable electrical loads and reduce energy wastage. Historical data is further used for AI-based energy prediction and predictive maintenance.

IoT Hardware Architecture

Suggested components for a safe SIH prototype demonstration using low-voltage sensors and suitable demonstration loads.

  SAFE PROTOTYPE DESIGN

ESP32

Main IoT controller. Reads sensors, communicates through Wi-Fi and sends monitoring data to the backend API.

PIR Sensor

Detects occupancy or motion in classrooms, labs and other monitored campus spaces.

DHT22 Sensor

Measures room temperature and humidity for comfort monitoring and cooling optimization.

LDR Sensor

Measures ambient light intensity to support automatic lighting decisions.

Energy Sensor

Provides energy and power usage information for equipment monitoring, analytics and anomaly detection.

Relay / Control Module

Controls suitable low-voltage demonstration loads to visually represent automation commands.

Safety note: This project interface is a software and presentation prototype. Do not use unsafe direct mains wiring. For SIH hardware demonstration, use proper isolation, qualified supervision and safe low-voltage or certified demonstration loads.
